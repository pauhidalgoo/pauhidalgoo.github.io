I’m Pau Hidalgo Pujol, an artificial-intelligence student and researcher from Banyoles, based in Barcelona. I have worked at [Nostrum Biodiscovery](https://www.nostrumbiodiscovery.com/) on geometric deep learning for protein structures.

I studied Artificial Intelligence at the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya. During a 2025–2026 exchange at ETH Zürich, I took master-level courses in probabilistic AI, neural-network theory, reliable and trustworthy AI, and learning in biological and artificial neural networks.

## Research interests

My main interests fall into three areas:

1. **Mathematical foundations of machine learning:** probability, optimisation, learning theory, generalisation, invariance, and representation learning.
2. **Geometric machine learning for biology:** graph neural networks, protein representation learning, graph construction, sparsification, and efficient scientific ML.
3. **Music, sound, and machine learning:** audio representations, symbolic music, generation, and computational creativity.

My bachelor’s thesis, completed with Telefónica I+D and UPC, studied whether eye-tracking signals can help train, evaluate, and interpret language models. Other projects include [Cucafera](/projects/cucafera/), a Catalan language model trained from scratch, and [Patufet](/projects/patufet/), its public dataset collection.
